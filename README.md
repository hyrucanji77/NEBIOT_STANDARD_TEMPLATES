# NEBIOT project and outcome templates — edition 1.2

Aligned with NEBIOT Standard 0.8.2. The standard itself is unchanged.

The seven document families are NIP, Prefeasibility, Feasibility, PD, ExAnte, ExPost, and Migration_Reconciliation, in American English (EN) and Spanish (ES).

- `Word/`: complete editable DOCX reports.
- `Word_Templates/`: reusable DOTX masters.
- `Overleaf/`: complete XeLaTeX project, original NEBIOT logos and optional stakeholder-logo fields.
- `PDF_Previews/`: compiled LaTeX reading copies.
- `Calculation_Workbooks/`: blank environmental accounts with formulas, notation and source mapping.
- `Registers/`: 81 annotated calculation-related tables per language and the common mathematical notation catalog.
- `Guides/`: English and Spanish completion and calculation-review instructions.
- `Tools/`: read-only original-formula replay and mathematical-expression export.

Open the corresponding `NEBIOT_<family>_<EN|ES>.tex` entry point in Overleaf and use XeLaTeX. Reports retain their stage-specific purpose; an added mathematical expression does not prescribe a new environmental method, parameter, entitlement or project duration.

Calculation formulas remain in their governing standard and qualified upstream records. The reports display native equations, table-level notation and a CALC traceability record. Workbook symbols are documented in Symbol_Map and Math_Notation. Source_Map and Reviewer_Match distinguish reproduction of the source from an explicitly justified receiving-method change.

Read the workbook-capacity instructions before entering a long time series. A blank field is not zero; an unqualified input is not evidence; an arithmetic match is not an OVV opinion or NVEU authorization.
